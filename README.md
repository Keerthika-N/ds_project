# Internship Selection Analysis

## Project Overview 
This project analyzes a dataset of 10,000 student records to identify the key drivers behind successful internship placements. The goal was to determine whether institutional factors (like College Tier) or individual performance metrics (like Interview Scores and Skills) carry more weight in the hiring process.

## Tech Stack
- Python (Pandas): Data cleaning, handling missing values, and boolean mapping.
- Excel: Pivot tables, cross-tabulation, and interactive data visualization.
- Statistical Analysis: Correlation analysis and success probability modeling.

## Key Insights
Through statistical analysis and Excel dashboarding, I identified three major findings:
- Merit Overcomes Prestige: High-performing students (Interview Score 8-10) from Tier 3 colleges achieved a 75.6% selection rate, outperforming lower-performing students from Tier 1 schools (73.7%).
- The "Success Driver": Interview Performance was the strongest predictor of selection, showing a higher correlation with success than CGPA or specific placement training.
- The Performance Lift: Moving from an Interview Score of 1 to a 10 resulted in a 9.3% increase in selection probability, proving the "high-stakes" nature of the interview process.

## Visualizations
- Selection Probability Trend: A line analysis showing the direct correlation between interview scores and hiring outcomes.
- Institutional ROI Matrix: A cross-tabulation comparing College Tiers against performance buckets.
